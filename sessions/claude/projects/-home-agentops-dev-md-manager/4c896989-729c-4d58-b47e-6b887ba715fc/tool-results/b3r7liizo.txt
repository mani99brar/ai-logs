+- `review` (`null` unless `review.json` exists): `attempt` (1), `transport` (`native` when `review.interactive.json` exists, else `print` when `automatic-review.json` exists, else `manual`), `reviewer_session_id` (`review.json` `reviewer`: the Claude session UUID or the operator-stated identity), `independent`, `bundle_sha256`, `candidate_commit`, `verdict` (`approved`/`blocked`), `findings[]` with `severity`, `message`, `disposition`, `worker` (`ui|adapter|both|none` or `null`) and `requirement` (verbatim quote or `null`; both `null` for reviews recorded before the reviewer prompt asked for them), `reviewed_at` (`automatic-review.json` `accepted_at`, else the last `review` event time, else the mtime of `review.json`; UTC with a trailing `Z`), and `diff` (`{path: "review.diff", sha256, bytes}` or `null`).
+- `inputs` (`null` when `policy.json` is missing): `feature`, `policy_version`, `base_commit`, `source_branch`, `mode` (`automatic` iff `plan.json` has `automatic`), `automatic` (`{finish, permission_mode, worker_timeout_seconds, review_timeout_seconds, reviewer_transport}` or `null`; for plans that predate `reviewer_transport` the export records the transport a recorded review actually used, `native` or `print`, and `null` when no review has happened, never a default), `setup[]` (`{argv, command, timeout_seconds}` with `command = shlex.join(argv)`), `max_verification_attempts`, `failure_drill`, and `workers.<ui|adapter>`: `role`, `task` (the plan's task text verbatim, including the appended ownership/checks JSON), `prompt` (`<worker>.prompt.txt` or `null` when the run did not record it), `owned_paths`, `checks[]` (`{id, kind, argv, command, timeout_seconds, scenarios}`), `launch` (the `<worker>.interactive.json` receipt: `session_id`, `launch_token`, `launch_requested_at` normalised to `Z`, `native_started_at` epoch ms, `observed_state`, `status`, `launcher_invocations`, `background_id`; or `null`), `completion` (`<worker>.completion.json` reduced to `{status, summary, open_assumptions}` or `null`), `handoff` (`{summary, open_assumptions}` or `null`) and `stop` (`{stopped, confirmed_at}` where `confirmed_at` is the stop file's mtime when stopped, else `null`).
+
+Task and prompt texts, finding messages and requirement quotes can contain absolute paths: the adapter redacts them and truncates oversized texts. Runs recorded under an older version keep loading; re-export them so the viewer shows their review and inputs:
+
+```bash
+.venv/bin/python -m workflow export "$HOME/.local/state/md-manager-workflows/project-workflows/project-workflows-001"
+```
+
+The command takes the controller lock, reads the checkpoint without invoking any node, constructs no sessions, launches nothing, refuses a run whose `plan.json`, pinned policy or `review.json` fail validation, and leaves `updated_at` alone when nothing changed.
