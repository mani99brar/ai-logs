## Decisions

- sea-compact-fixes-001's general reviewer approved the candidate `df8fad0`. The coverage reviewer blocked it with a P1: the approach sweep skipped lanes. This follow-up builds on the approved candidate (branch `sea-compact/fixes-base`), without a snapshot restore. The new reviewers see only the fix.
- The fix is a generous rule, not a longer reach: a ship on the berth tile or any of its eight neighbours moors when E is pressed, even across a corner. Right beside the dock means docked. The reach stays 384, and a berth behind land (not adjacent) still doesn't moor.
- The sweep must account for every lane, with no silent skips and no count floors, so a docking dead zone can't hide again.
- The sketch writer moves into the typechecked `scripts/build-map.ts`, and the pure generator is checked for `node:` imports: the coverage reviewer's P2.

- Corrected after design challenge attempt 1 (its alternative, adopted): the rule lives in `dockInReach`, so the HUD hint agrees with it, and it has a diagonal-pinch guard. The diagonal half of the old corner test flips. The behind-land negative test uses a larger test reach, because at the shipped 384 the neighbourhood covers the whole reach.

- Corrected after design challenge attempt 2 (its alternative, adopted): the sweep checks each step against a terrain-only oracle, rather than classifying lanes up front from straight-line geometry (ships slide along coasts). The never-mooring lanes are an exact hard-coded list, and the loop also runs over every real dock.

- Corrected after design challenge attempt 3:
  - The real south docks had a second dead zone caused by a land spur at (20–21, 28) and its mirrors. The spurs are removed or moved away from the docks' approaches, so docks are easy to reach, which is better play than listing the lanes as acceptable failures.
  - The sweep's lanes are defined by sea start positions on a grid around each dock, keyed by dock, so no lane starts on land and none is skipped.
