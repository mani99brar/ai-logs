# Task: game

## Goal

Make the islands' rubbings something you earn with your head, not just your hands. Each regular island's sources start **locked** behind its ruin (§3). A captain moored at the island opens the ruin and reads the clue at the site:
- **a three-symbol sequence:** six carved tablets with relational hints;
- **a small navigation puzzle:** a 4 × 4 grid.

They enter the answer. The first player to solve it unlocks the island's rubbings for everyone, and every solver earns 40 gold once. A wrong answer locks that player out for 3 s. A fresh ruin should take about 20–40 s including the 6 s rubbing, so the UI must make the clue easy to read and the answer quick to enter, and guessing must be worse than reading. The pure rules are on `main` (`packages/simulation/src/ruins`, `docs/ruins.md`); this feature wires them in.

## Design (settled)

- **Seeds:** the ruins get their own seed derived from the match's master secret, the `match-online` mechanism. This fixes the ruins reviewer's finding that `seed ^ const` let clues leak the ledger seed.
- **Locking:** at match start every ledger source is locked (`rubbings-theft` unlocked them all as a stopgap; remove that). Collection at an island needs its ruin solved by anyone: the first solve's `sources-unlocked` calls `unlockSource` for each.
- **Interaction:** moored at an island whose ruin this player hasn't solved, pressing **R** (a new key, shown in the HUD) opens the ruin panel. `openRuin` returns only the clue. Casting off, entering a duel or disconnecting closes the session, as the rules define. The panel shows the clue clearly:
  - **sequence:** six tablets with their symbols and hint sentences, and the answer entered by clicking three symbols in order, or keys 1–6, with an undo;
  - **navigation:** the grid drawn with start, goal and blocked cells, and the answer entered with the arrow keys or WASD, shown as a path, with an undo.
  - Submit sends `answer-ruin`: zod-validated, rate-limited, idempotent by request id.
  - A wrong answer shows the 3 s lockout counting down.
  - Solving shows "Ruin solved: +40 gold", and, if first, "The island's rubbings are unlocked".
- **Gold:** every solver's `gold-awarded` calls the ledger's `grantGold` (reason `ruin`, key `String(island)`), so it's once per player per ruin by the ledger's own keys.
- **HUD:** at an island, the ruin state (locked, unlocked, solved by you), the R prompt, and then the familiar "hold E to collect".
