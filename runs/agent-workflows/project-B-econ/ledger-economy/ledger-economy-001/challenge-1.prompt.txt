# Task: ledger

## Goal

Extend the pure objective ledger with §11's small match economy, so later features can wire it in:
- gold minted by the rules for ruin completions and supply caches;
- dockside purchases (a targeted rumor for 25 gold, a sail tonic for 30) with idempotent request ids;
- the sail-tonic inventory and its one active effect.

The economy's job is route choices and recovery, not a second race, so it must be exploit-proof: no double spends, no farming, no minting outside the rules, and gold always accounted for.

## Design (settled)

- **Minted gold.** `grantGold(state, { playerId, reason, sourceKey, tick })`, with `reason` either `ruin` (40 gold) or `supply-cache` (25 gold); the amounts come from content.
  - `goldIssued` grows by the amount, so the invariant "balances sum to `goldIssued`" still holds.
  - It is idempotent by its key: a `ruin` grant is once per (player, `sourceKey`), where the ruin id is the key; a `supply-cache` grant is once per `sourceKey` for the whole match (one global claim, §11).
  - A repeat returns `duplicate` or `already-claimed` with the same state. The caller can't choose the amount.
- **Purchases.** `purchase(state, { requestId, playerId, item, tick })`, with `item` either `rumor` (25) or `tonic` (30); the prices come from content.
  - A `requestId` is unique per match. A repeat returns the **original** result with the same state, so a retry never double-spends (§11).
  - It is refused when gold is short, when the player is in a duel (reserved) or has left, or, for a tonic, when the player already carries two.
  - Gold is spent, not moved to another player: the ledger tracks it as `goldSpent`, and the invariant becomes "balances + `goldSpent` = `goldIssued`".
  - The rumor itself is delivered by the caller (the intel module) after a successful purchase. The documented flow checks availability first, so a rumor that can't deliver is never bought.
- **Tonics.**
  - Each player carries at most two (a count).
  - `useTonic(state, { playerId, tick, atSea })` activates one for 12 s (from content): refused while one is active, while in a duel, or when `atSea` is false.
  - The caller also refuses it while the player is being grappled, and passes that as a flag.
  - Losing a duel keeps the tonics (§11). Leaving the match drops them.
  - The ledger exposes `activeTonic(state, playerId, tick)`. Its sailing effect (+10 % speed) is applied later by the sailing code, so the ledger never touches movement.
- The existing duel settlement, caches and channels are unchanged.

## Acceptance

1. **Grants:** scripted tests:

2. **Purchases:** scripted tests for each refusal (short gold, in a duel, left, a third tonic), the prices, and a repeated `requestId` returning the original result without charging again, including after other operations in between. # Decisions: ledger-economy

From the overnight session of 2026-09-24/25. The operator is asleep and asked the assistant to decide, record each decision, and use the workflow for the code.

## Decisions

- The economy goes into the existing pure ledger, so gold stays in one place with one invariant.
- The ledger tracks spent gold as `goldSpent`, so the invariant becomes "balances + spent = issued". Minted gold comes only through keyed grants (ruin once per player per ruin; supply cache once per match), so nothing can be farmed.
- Purchases carry request ids. A retry returns the original result and never charges twice (§11).
- The rumor is bought only after the intel module says one is available, so players never pay for nothing (pairs with intel-core's request outcomes).
- Tonics: carry at most two, one active, 12 s, at sea only, not in a duel or while grappled; kept on a duel loss, dropped on leaving. The ledger only tracks them; the sailing code applies the +10 % later.

## Assumptions

- The Haki focus scroll (75 gold) is alpha-only (§11) and stays out.
- Shops and supply-cache locations and interaction times are the caller's (world wiring) concern.

## Deferred

- Wiring grants, purchases and tonics into the match room, the tonic sailing effect, the shop UI: `objectives-endgame`.
